2-Week Meal Planner

A general low-carb, high-protein plan with Filipino-friendly dishes. Carbohydrate sits at breakfast so lunch and dinner stay light — no rice, just cauliflower rice, extra vegetables, or a bigger portion of protein.

Reference

Ground rules
  • No rice at lunch and dinner. Swap in cauliflower rice, double the vegetables, or take a larger portion of protein.
  • Protein at every meal — roughly a palm-and-a-half sized portion, 40–50 g per main meal.
  • Carbs live at breakfast — oats, fruit, and smoothies early in the day, not late.
  • Vegetables fill half the plate at lunch and dinner. Leafy greens, sitaw, sayote, kalabasa, pechay, kangkong, cabbage.
  • Cook with real fat — olive or coconut oil, whole eggs, avocado, nuts. Skip margarine and fried street food.
  • Water first. 3–4 litres a day. No soft drinks, no sweetened juice, no powdered iced tea.
  • Sauces are the hidden carb. Banana ketchup, sweet chilli, hoisin, and gravy add up fast — use vinegar, calamansi, soy, garlic, and chilli instead.
Fruit allowance

Two servings a day, taken at breakfast or as a morning snack — not at night.

Best choices

  • Berries — strawberry, blueberry
  • Papaya (1 thick slice)
  • Apple (1 medium)
  • Guava (2 small)
  • Pomelo (3–4 segments)
  • Watermelon (1 cup)

In moderation

  • Banana — lakatan or saba (1 small)
  • Mango (half a medium)
  • Pineapple (1 slice)
  • Orange (1 medium)

Skip

  • Dried fruit, fruit juice, canned fruit in syrup
  • Lanzones, chico, and durian in quantity
Snack ideas

One snack a day is optional — take it only if you are genuinely hungry between meals. Every option here leads with protein or fat, not starch.

  • Two hard-boiled eggs with a pinch of salt
  • A small handful of almonds, peanuts, or cashews (about 30 g)
  • Greek yogurt with a few berries
  • String cheese with apple slices
  • Protein shake mixed with water
  • Cucumber and carrot sticks with hummus
  • A small banana with a tablespoon of peanut butter

Practical notes

Prep

Cook proteins in bulk over the weekend — a tray of chicken, a pot of adobo, two dozen boiled eggs. Rice cauliflower in a food processor and freeze it in portions. Wash and cut vegetables the night before so a weekday meal is ten minutes, not forty.

Eating out

Order grilled or sinigang over fried and sweet. Ask for the rice to be left off — most carinderias and restaurants will swap in extra vegetables. Inasal without the rice and with extra atchara is a reliable default.

Alcohol

Beer is liquid carbohydrate. If you drink, keep it to two servings a week and choose spirits with soda water and calamansi over beer or sweet mixers.

Tracking progress

Weigh in once a week, same day, same time, after waking and before eating. The scale moves unevenly — the trend over three weeks is the signal, not any single morning.
